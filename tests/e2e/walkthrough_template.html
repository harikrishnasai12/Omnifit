    Findings

    What the recording showed

    Read straight from the three runs above.

    Worked well

    • One sentence became a plan. Priya said “my sister's wedding is on 14 December”. The app saved the event, opened her feed with a 61-day countdown and three looks tagged “For your Sister's wedding on 14 Dec”, and put the wedding first in her stories.
    • Mixed languages understood. Meena wrote in Hinglish. The app picked out Diwali, traditional, under ₹4,000 and her age (from her own words), then asked one question before styling.
    • Corrections win. After Meena's “Not me: white”, the look shown “Because you loved 4 white looks” left her feed.
    • Guests keep their data. Arjun's love as a guest stored nothing on the server. His phone re-ranked the feed on its own.
    • Scrolling needs no AI. Feed pages came back in 50–450 ms, because the looks are built ahead of time. Gemma runs only when someone asks something.

    Needs fixing

    • Arjun's chat didn't reach his feed. He asked for college wear under ₹1,500 as a guest, but the feed didn't use it. A guest's intent should stay on the phone and go with each feed request, and move to the account at sign-up.
    • Festivals crowd out personal taste. Five of Arjun's seven first cards said “Ready for Navratri”. Festival looks should be capped per page.
    • “Loved 4” overcounts. Priya loved 3 blue looks, but saves and long looks also counted. The wording should become “Like the blue looks you loved”.
    • Too few wedding looks. Priya's second visit had no wedding looks left: the pool has only 3 women's wedding looks. The builder should run longer overnight.
    • Slow answers while the GPU is shared. Chat answers took 62–78 s during the run (with a free GPU it's usually under 30 s). The feed isn't affected.
